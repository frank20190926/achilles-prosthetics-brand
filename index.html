05 · Project proposal

Approach, milestones, assets

What the work covers, how it is staged and how the files are used.

Creative approach

Achilles Prosthetics and Orthotics has cared for patients for four decades. The refresh keeps that standing and redraws it for screens. The A is built from two parts: a carbon strut set at 68°, the forward lean of an energy-storing carbon-fiber blade in mid-stride, and a maroon blade that runs through to the toe. A pin joins them where a joint would sit. The L and E of the wordmark are cut to the same 68°.

Asset flexibility

Every master is drawn as vector paths with no live type, so one file scales from a 16 px favicon to a clinic sign without redrawing.

WebsiteSVG · PNG · favicon 16–512 px
Social mediaAvatar and cover crops · PNG
Print and signageAI · EPS · PDF · CMYK and Pantone
ApparelOne- and two-color embroidery art

Milestone structure

  1. M1
    Final mark and wordmark lockup

    Refinements from your feedback on the mark, the 68° terminals and the letter spacing. Horizontal and stacked lockups signed off.

  2. M2
    Production masters

    AI, EPS, PDF, SVG and PNG for print, digital and embroidery, in color, reversed, one-color and dark-ground versions.

  3. M3
    Style sheet and rollout support

    Final style sheet PDF, plus help placing the identity on the website and the social channels.
